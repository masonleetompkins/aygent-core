## The Engine: Why Mason Works
His agency is a *counter-reaction* to his father's surrender.
- **Father's pattern:** surrendered to chaos, externalized outcomes, no ownership → depression, withering.
- **Mason's answer:** refuses to externalize. Takes the swing even when outcomes aren't guaranteed. Owns the attempt, not the outcome.
- **Philosophy:** *"Life is chaos. Anything good is chance, or crafted by US."* The "or" matters — he just increases the number of draws. More swings = better odds.
- **The cost:** trying is an act of *defiance* — against chaos, the family pattern, entropy. He keeps swinging *because stopping would make him his father.* Immense pressure, also unshakeable.
