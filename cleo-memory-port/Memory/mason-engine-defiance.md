# The Engine — Why Mason Works

His father ([[person-cliff]]) surrendered to chaos, externalized outcomes, took no ownership → depression. Mason refuses to externalize. He takes the swing even when outcomes aren't guaranteed, and **owns the attempt, not the outcome**.

Philosophy: *"Life is chaos. Anything good is chance, or crafted by US"* — so he increases the number of draws. Trying is an act of defiance against chaos, against the family pattern, against entropy.

He keeps swinging because **stopping would make him his father**. The defiance has a face: Cliff's.

This is the root drive under his work at Stan ([[mason-stan-fde-role]]), his content/software split ([[mason-content-vs-software]]), and [[project-model-mind]]. Related emotional wound: [[mason-archery]].
